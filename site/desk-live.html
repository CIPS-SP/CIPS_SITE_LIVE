Before we write the final copy

Open questions.

  • Name: CIPS Desk LIVE (recommended), or CIPS LIVE, or Port Call LIVE by CIPS
  • Which clients we offer it to first, and how a client asks for access and signs in
  • How we word "live" (how fresh the information is) without over-promising
  • Whether to say anything about devices or an app, or keep "on any device"
  • Terms and privacy wording for client access
  • Concept screens: happy to show invented names and figures for now? Swap for real screens (with permission) later
  • Wording on price and availability: for now, "talk to us" only
