Privacy notice.

How we collect, use and look after personal information when you use this website, send us an enquiry or review a port call.

Last updated 7 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by St Helier Port Services Limited, trading as Channel Island Port Services ("CIPS", "we", "us"), a company registered in Jersey under number 33656, whose registered office is at 9 Bond Street, St Helier, Jersey JE2 3NP.

We are the controller of the personal information described in this notice. We are registered with the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner under registration number 65432.

If you have any question about this notice or your information, contact us at admin@cips.agency, or write to us at the address above.

The law we follow

We handle personal information under the Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018. Where it applies to visitors in the European Union or the United Kingdom, we also follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation and the UK GDPR.

What we collect and why

1. Enquiries

When you press "Send enquiry", the form sends what you entered (your name, how to contact you, details such as a vessel, port, dates, company, cargo or project, and your message) to us. We use it to reply to you, to prepare quotes and to arrange the services you ask for.

  • Your enquiry goes first to the same small "drop box" service we run on Cloudflare's network for reviews. It is saved there before the page tells you it is on its way to us, so it cannot be lost on the way.
  • The drop box then emails it to our duty agent at dutyagent@cips.agency using Brevo, an email delivery service based in France. If you gave an email address, our reply goes to it. The drop box can only email us; it never emails you or anyone else.
  • The drop box keeps the enquiry for 30 days, so that we can recover it if an email goes astray, and then deletes it. Our copy is the email we receive.
  • As for reviews, we do not store your IP address: a scrambled (hashed) version is kept for two hours to stop abuse.
  • If the form cannot reach us, it tells you so and offers to open your own email app instead; nothing reaches us unless you send that email.

Our legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries about our business.

2. Email, phone and WhatsApp

If you email, call or message us, we use your contact details and what you tell us to answer you and to provide our services. If you contact us on WhatsApp, the message also passes through WhatsApp, which is run by WhatsApp Ireland Limited / Meta under its own privacy policy.

Our legal basis: performing a contract with you, or our legitimate interest in answering you.

3. Port call reviews

After a port call we may send you a private link to review it. The link carries the details of that call (the vessel's name, port, dates and services) so the review page can show them; it never shows your booking record.

On the review page you can give ratings, say who is answering (for example Captain or Chef), add your name if you wish (it is optional), write comments, and choose whether we may name your ship if we publish the review.

  • Your answers go first to a small "drop box" service we run on Cloudflare's network, and from there into our own booking system.
  • We check every review against a real booking before publishing it. Published reviews are anonymous unless you tick the box allowing your ship's name. We never publish your name or your contact details.
  • We do not store your IP address. To stop abuse, the drop box keeps a scrambled (hashed) version of it for two hours, then deletes it.

Our legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving our service and showing honest, verified feedback; and your consent for naming your ship, which you can withdraw at any time by contacting us.

4. Visiting the website

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not set cookies. The site loads nothing from other companies' servers (no outside fonts, maps, videos or "like" buttons); everything you see comes from our own site.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, necessarily processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site to you and protect it from attack. It handles this under its own data processing terms.

A few small settings, such as the departure you choose on the globe, are kept in your own browser so the site can remember them during your visit. They are never sent to us. See our cookies and storage notice.

Our legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website.

Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for marketing. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who work on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect it: Cloudflare (website hosting and the drop box for enquiries and reviews), Brevo (delivering website enquiries to us by email), Airtable (our booking and review records), Render (the system we use to manage reviews and port calls), and Microsoft (our email, Microsoft 365, provided through GoDaddy).
  • Port authorities, customs, immigration and suppliers, where this is needed to deliver the port services you ask us for.
  • Authorities when the law requires it.

Information sent outside Jersey

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Jersey. When they do, it is protected in one of these ways:

  • In the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, which Jersey law treats as giving adequate protection: Brevo (France) and WhatsApp (Ireland).
  • In the United States, with companies certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension), which Jersey recognises as giving adequate protection for certified companies: Cloudflare, Microsoft and Render.
  • Elsewhere, under other safeguards the law recognises, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in the provider's data processing terms.

You can ask us for more detail about these safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and correspondence: up to 3 years if no business follows; if it does, they become part of the booking records below.
  • Booking and port call records: 10 years, as Jersey law requires for accounting records.
  • Reviews: answers waiting in the drop box are removed once collected into our booking system. Reviews in our records are kept with the booking they belong to, and deleted with it; a published review stays on the website until it is no longer relevant or you ask us to remove it.
  • Hashed IP addresses for abuse protection: two hours.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018 and the GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it if it is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete it, or to limit how we use it;
  • object to our using it on the basis of our legitimate interests;
  • ask for information you gave us in a portable form;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it (for example, to name your ship in a published review).

To use any of these rights, contact us at admin@cips.agency. We will reply within the time the law allows, normally within four weeks.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner (jerseyoic.org), or to the data protection authority where you live or work.

Keeping it safe

We use secure, encrypted connections for the website, the enquiry form and the review page, limit access to the people who need it, and choose providers with strong security. Our drop box keeps only the fields an enquiry or review needs, and refuses anything else.

Children

Our services are for businesses and the crews of vessels. The website is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this notice
