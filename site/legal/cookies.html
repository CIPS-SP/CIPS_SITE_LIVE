Cookies and storage.

Short version: we set no cookies, use no analytics or advertising, and track nobody. That is why you do not see a cookie banner.

Last updated 6 November 2026

No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set any cookies, and it does not use analytics, advertising, social media plug-ins or any other tracking. It loads nothing from other companies' servers while you browse.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may use strictly necessary security measures to protect the site from attack; these do not track you across other websites.

Settings kept in your browser

So that the site works the way you expect, a few small settings are kept in your own browser's storage. They stay on your device, are never sent to us or to anyone else, and contain nothing that identifies you. Most are forgotten as soon as you close the tab.

Because these settings are needed for features you choose to use and never leave your device, they do not need your consent. You can clear them at any time in your browser's settings (look for "clear site data" or "cookies and site data"); the site will simply start fresh.

Links to other websites

Some links take you to other websites, such as WhatsApp, LinkedIn or Yachting Pages. Those websites have their own cookie and privacy policies, which apply once you are there.
