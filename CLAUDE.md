## The rules of this repo
- `site/` is everything public and **must be self-contained: no request to any outside server** (no CDNs, fonts, tiles, images, APIs, analytics). The allowed exceptions: the CIPS review drop box address in `site/review/config.js`, and Cloudflare Web Analytics (17 Nov 2026, Simon: cookie-free visit counts; switched on in the Pages project, which adds its script on the live site only; allowed in the CSP by `tools/csp.mjs`; described on the Cookies and Privacy pages). Links people click are fine.
- No `../` paths out of `site/`; no files the pages do not use (no notes, tools, backups, old versions); every file under 24 MiB.
- The globe shows **no ships and no flights, and no AIS / flights pills** (4 Nov 2026, Simon: switched off, we may bring them back). The switch is `TRAFFIC: false` in the `options.ais` of `site/index.html` (read in `_beats/globe-live/live.js`); set it to `true` to bring back the simulated fleet, the simulated flights and both pills. They are simulated either way (`window.CIPS_LIVE_DATA = false`): never describe them as live.
- After changes: `cd tools && npm run tailwind` if classes changed, `node csp.mjs` if any inline `<script>` changed (the security headers allow each by fingerprint), then `node check.mjs` (about 15 minutes for everything; flags `--desktop --phone --pages --doors`). It must report no missing files, no errors, nothing outside.
- Verify in a browser before saying something works (headless Chrome via the check, or the browser pane).

## The visitor's vessel (done 4 Nov 2026)
`site/js/vessel.js` holds where the visitor is sailing from (Mediterranean = Monaco, Northern Europe = Rotterdam, Caribbean = St Maarten; Mediterranean default) and an optional vessel name (Kinship default; the picker stopped asking for one on phones 14 Nov and on computers 20 Nov 2026, Simon), kept for the session, announced on `window` as a `cips:vessel` event. The globe's chooser panel (`site/js/globe-enhancements.js`, `arrangeDeparture`) sets it; the globe (`_beats/globe-live/beat.js`, `syncVesselFields`) replans the demo vessel's route and card title from it; the chart scene's tag, the enquiry form's vessel field and the arrival mini-game (`_beats/seaway-wake/game.js`, `YACHT`: the pilot, Coastguard and VTS use the name, and the vessel is "under way from" the chosen port) read it. The berthing and hold-the-line games never named the vessel, so nothing was needed there. "Observe the Channel Islands" and the ship list are gone (hidden, not deleted: the picker code still exists behind them).

The picker carries a **preview of the journey card** (title, "N nm from <place>", destination, what for, speed, ETA) under the choices; it opens on the Mediterranean and follows each choice and the name. Its numbers come from the route the globe plans: `beat.js` (`publishKinRoute`) puts them on `CIPS_VESSEL.route` and fires `cips:route`; the real voyage card in the panel uses the same route (ETA counts down along it). Choosing a departure, or opening the picker, turns the globe to it (`focusOn`); once the journey is under way `tick()` keeps the globe turned to the vessel all the way (as it already did for a routed ship). "Scroll to start the journey" (the first scroll presses it) folds the picker away (`.voyage-mode`) and leaves the card with "Scroll to continue".
