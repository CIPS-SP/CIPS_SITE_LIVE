Cookies and storage.

Short version: we set no cookies, use no advertising, and track nobody. We count visits with Cloudflare's cookie-free analytics. That is why you do not see a cookie banner.

Last updated 6 November 2026

No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set any cookies, and it does not use advertising, social media plug-ins or any tracking that follows you. To see how many people visit and which pages they read, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics: it sets no cookies, keeps nothing on your device, does not identify you or follow you to other websites, and gives us only totals (pages viewed, the website you came from, country, and browser and device type). Apart from that small Cloudflare script, the site loads nothing from other companies' servers while you browse.

On the home page, the journey also notes a few milestones (for example "started the journey", "reached the quay" or "went to the main site") with our own small CIPS service on Cloudflare, which keeps only a total for each day. Nothing about you, your device or your address is sent with them or kept.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may use strictly necessary security measures to protect the site from attack; these do not track you across other websites.

Settings kept in your browser

So that the site works the way you expect, a few small settings are kept in your own browser's storage. They stay on your device, are never sent to us or to anyone else, and contain nothing that identifies you. Most are forgotten as soon as you close the tab.

Because these settings are needed for features you choose to use and never leave your device, they do not need your consent. You can clear them at any time in your browser's settings (look for "clear site data" or "cookies and site data"); the site will simply start fresh.

Links to other websites

Some links take you to other websites, such as WhatsApp, LinkedIn or Yachting Pages. Those websites have their own cookie and privacy policies, which apply once you are there.
